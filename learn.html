All Lessons

Thirteen lessons in four worlds. Each one has learning goals, diagrams, worked math, code, and a quiz at the end.

WORLD 1 Foundations

How a token is actually produced, what a GPU is good and bad at, and how to measure an inference system.

How LLM Inference Works

Tokens, the autoregressive loop, prefill vs decode, and sampling.

4 topics~20 min
1-2

GPU Fundamentals & the Roofline

Memory hierarchy, arithmetic intensity, and why decode is memory-bound.

4 topics~30 min
1-3

Metrics: Latency, Throughput & Cost

TTFT, ITL, throughput, goodput, percentiles and cost per token.

4 topics~20 min

WORLD 2 Core Techniques

The four ideas every serving engine is built on: KV caching, batching, fast attention and quantization.

2-1

The KV Cache

Why we cache keys and values, how big the cache gets, and how to shrink it.

4 topics~30 min
2-2

Batching: Static → Continuous

Static, dynamic and continuous batching, plus chunked prefill.

4 topics~30 min
2-3

Attention Optimizations

MQA/GQA/MLA, FlashAttention and PagedAttention.

4 topics~35 min
2-4

Quantization

INT8, FP8, INT4 and FP4; weight-only vs weight+activation; GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant.

4 topics~35 min

Squeezing more out of the hardware: speculative decoding, multi-GPU parallelism, prefix reuse and smart scheduling.

3-1

Speculative Decoding

3-3

Prefix Caching & KV Memory

3-4

Scheduling & Disaggregated Serving

Schedulers, prefill/decode disaggregation, routing and autoscaling.

4 topics~30 min

Writing and fusing kernels, then running the whole thing reliably in production.

Kernels, Fusion & Compilation

Kernel fusion, CUDA graphs, Triton, torch.compile and profiling.

4 topics~35 min
4-2

Serving in Production

Engines, capacity planning, benchmarking, observability and a launch checklist.

4 topics~35 min
